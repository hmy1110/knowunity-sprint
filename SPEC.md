# Spec: voice active-recall prototype

This prototype is the Next.js app in this repo (`src/app/`). Every screen listed below is a page with its own route, reached by the student clicking through the flow — not a Storybook story. Storybook stays the component catalog only: it's where each component's states get built and screenshotted in isolation, never where the student navigates.

## What we're building

A mocked, voice-in/text-out active-recall loop: a student explains 4 art&design terms out loud (or types, if they can't speak), Knowie replies in text with a hardcoded verdict per term, and a summary shows how each one was answered. No real speech-to-text, no real judging, no voice output.

## Current behavior (2026-09-21) — read this first

Mia turned the fixed script into a freer prototype. Where the sections below still describe the old script (term 4 Skipped, Skip wired only on term 4, the review as an all-correct pass, Summary as fixed data, the study plan state from `?state=`), this section wins. The Figma frame history in those sections is still accurate.

**What is still mocked:** no real speech-to-text or judging. The outcome of a normal attempt is decided by term number, not by what was said or typed: term 1 Recalled, 2 Hinted, 3 Revealed, 4 Recalled.

**Session (`/session`)**
- Every term can be answered by voice or by typing. The input mode sticks: after typing, the next term opens on the typing screen until the person taps "Switch to voice" or records; a recording flips it back to voice. `/session?entry=text` (Primer's text-mode entries) starts in typing.
- "I don't know" is Revealed (on the voice `idle`, and on a Hinted result). Top-right Skip is Skipped and is live only before an attempt (the voice `idle` and the typing screen); after that it is in Button's Disabled state (`Steps` `skipDisabled`).
- Hinted (term 2 only): the retry follows the input mode. After a voice first attempt it is the mic "Try again" (with "Type instead" and "I don't know"); after a typed one it is a Primary "Try again" that opens the typed retry, with "Switch to voice" and "I don't know". A voice first attempt with a typed retry (and the reverse) is one layout: the history renders one row per attempt, an audio scrubber or the typed text.
- Typed states with no Figma frame, built as the voice frames with the typed text in place of the audio: `typeResultHinted`, `typeRetryInput`, `typeRetryProcessing`, `typeResultHinted1Recalled`, `typeResultRevealed`. They are logged in `component-gaps.md`.
- "Resume" on `readyToSend` returns to the ordinary `recording` screen and carries the same take on (the recorder is paused, not stopped); no new visual.
- Status bar, appBar, `Steps` and the bottom content are fixed in the 390 x 844 frame; only the middle scrolls, and it scrolls to the newest row on each new screen.
- Each result is recorded as its screen appears (`src/lib/recall-session.ts`, sessionStorage, dies with the tab). A first run resets the record when `/session` mounts.

**Review run (`/session?review=1`)** covers the terms not yet recalled (terms 2-4 when nothing is recorded). A normal attempt is Recalled; Skip and "I don't know" work as in a first run, so a review can end with terms still missed. Its Summary is `/summary?variant=review` (`all-recalled` is the older name).

**Summary (`/summary`)** reads the record: rows, counts, percent (Recalled ÷ total), XP (2 per Recalled) and headline all follow it. The first-run Summary lists all four terms; a review Summary lists only the terms that run covered, with what each really ended on. Headline (`summaryHeadline`, four tiers as of 2026-09-23): "Nice work, Mia!" when every listed term was Recalled (the `Summary-all recalled` frame, with a single Continue), "Let’s go again, Mia." when none was, "Almost there, Mia." for more than half but not all, "Good session, Mia." otherwise — so 3 of 4 no longer reads exactly like 1 of 4. Both Figma-confirmed pairings are unchanged (4 of 4, and 1 of 4 on the mixed frame). A review Summary’s percent caption reads "recalled in this review" instead of Figma’s "recalled this session", since its percent is out of the terms that run covered, not the session; and its Recalled rows are titled "Recalled on review", not "Recalled on your own", since every term a review covers had already failed to come back unaided (`TermResultList`’s `run` prop). All three are copy-only — no count, percent, XP or BLAZING value moved. See `component-gaps.md`. BLAZING is derived, not timed: Recalled 0:20, Hinted 0:30, Revealed 0:15, Skipped 0:04 per term, and 0:43 per Recalled term in a review (so the two Figma frames still read 1:09 and 2:09). "Review what you missed" shows whenever a term is not Recalled. Status bar, appBar and the buttons are fixed; the content between scrolls. Opened cold with nothing recorded it shows Figma's own sample.

**Study plan (`/`)**: the state is derived from how many terms have ever been recalled (first run plus review): 0 `notStarted`, 1-3 `inProgress` with "N OF 4" and a bar at N/4, 4 `finish`. `?state=` still overrides it, and `?state=notStarted` clears the record. After closing the session early, the `inProgress` button reads "Continue" instead of "Review" (both open the same review run). The card has a 2px `pro/bold` outline in `notStarted` and `inProgress`, none at `finish`. The mascot peeks out from behind the card, unclipped on the left.

**Removed:** the hotspot-hint layer (`HotspotHints.tsx` and every `data-hotspot*` attribute) went away once every control became live.
